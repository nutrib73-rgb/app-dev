#The Hangover
  
*The Hangover is a comedy movie released in 2009. It was directed by Todd Phillips and tells the story of four friends who travel to Las Vegas for a bachelor party.*

**Main Characters**
1. Phil Wenneck
2. Stu Price
3. Alan Garner
4. Doug Billings

**Why I Like It?**
- The movie is very funny.
- The story is full of surprises.
- The characters have great chemistry.
- "What happens in Vegas, stays in Vegas."

**Favorite Scene**
My favorite scene is when the group tries to figure out what happened during their wild night in Las Vegas. It is both funny and exciting.

**Rating**
9/10

**Fun Fact**
A real tiger was used in some scenes of the movie.
